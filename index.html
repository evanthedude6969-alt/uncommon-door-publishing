Traditional Care, Modern Tools, Affordable Publishing

At Uncommon Door Publishing:

We believe a great story does not need explicit content, graphic violence, excessive profanity, or crude humor to capture a readers imagination. We are committed to publishing clean, engaging books while giving writers the freedom to explore meaningful stories across many genres.

Our books may make you laugh, keep you up turning pages, take you on an adventure, explore love and relationships, or tackle difficult issues. We welcome stories about imperfect people living in an imperfect world. "Clean does not mean shallow, and wholesome does not mean boring."

Our standard is simple: tell a great story, tell it well, and leave the reader with something worth carrying beyond the final page.

For Writers:

At Uncommon Door, we believe every manuscript deserves more than being uploaded, processed, and praised simply because someone paid a fee. We want to read your story.

We encourage traditional bound manuscript submissions because sometimes there is no substitute for holding a story in your hands, turning its pages, writing in margins, and getting to know the person behind the words.

We embrace modern technology, including AI as another tool in the publishing tool box. It can assist with editing, formatting, design, and production- but technology should support the storyteller, never replace the storyteller. Your story should still be you.

Relationship with Authors:

At Uncommon Door, paying for publishing services does not mean buying our praise. We believe an honest relationship with an author sometimes requires saying, "This isnt ready yet." If we see problems with a manuscript, we will tell you what we see and work with you toward making the story stronger.

The author always remains the storyteller. Our job is not to rewrite your voice into ours, but help you develop, prepare, and present your work professionally while preserving what made the story yours in the first place.

We would rather help you publish the right book later than rush the wrong book today.
