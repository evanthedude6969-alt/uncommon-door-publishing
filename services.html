Publishing Options:

Every author comes to us from a different place. Some have a finished manuscript and simply need help getting it into readers hands. Others may have an exceptional story but not the financial resources to publish it. Uncommon Door believes both deserve a possibility.

Author-Funded Publishing:

For authors who want to invest in their own work, Uncommon Door offers affordable publishing assistance without taking ownership of your story. Services may include manuscript evaluation, editing assistance, interior formatting, cover preparation, print and ebook setup, distribution assistance, and guidance through the publication process. Choose only what you need. We will never require you to purchase services your book may not require.

Cover Design & Preparation - $125.00

A great story deserves a cover that represents it well. Uncommon Ground will work with the author to develop a professional front cover, spine, and back cover prepared to the specifications required by the selected publishing platform. This includes placement of text, author photo when desired, publisher information, and space for ISBN/barcode. This service includes up to two reasonable rounds of revisions and delivery of print-ready cover file.

Specialty artwork, commissioned illustration through Noah Pilcher Studio when available, or licensing costs are not included and will always be discussed with the author before additional costs are incurred.

Publishing Setup & Distribution - $100.00

A finished book still needs a path to its readers. Uncommon Ground will assist with setting up your completed book for print and/or ebook distribution through appropriate publishing platforms. This includes uploading approved files, entering book information and metadata, selecting categories, establishing pricing, and reviewing the digital proof with the author before publication. The Author will be informed of third-party costs, including ISBNs, printing, proofs, or distribution fees, before those expenses are incurred.

Final Publication will never be approved without the authors consent.

Copy Editing & Proofreading - $175.00 Up to 75,000 words or 300 manuscript pages

Every story deserves a careful final reading. Uncommon Door will review the manuscript for spelling, grammar, punctuation, word usage, consistency, and obvious errors that could distract the reader. We will also flag passages that may need clarification while working to preserve the authors natural voice and style. This service includes one complete editing pass and one reasonable correction review after the author has considered the suggested changes. The Author retains approval of all editorial changes.

Manuscripts exceeding 75,000 words or 300 pages will be individually quoted before work begins.

Developmental editing, substantial rewriting, or major restructuring of a manuscript is not included in this service.

Developmental Editing - Starting at $250.00

Sometimes a manuscript needs more than correction- it needs development. Developmental editing looks at the larger story, including plot structure, pacing, character development, continuity, dialog, unnecessary or missing scenes, and areas where the story may not be communicating what the author intended.

Rather than rewriting the book for you, Uncommon Ground will provide detailed recommendations and work with you as you revise your story in your own voice.

Because developmental editing varies considerably from one manuscript to another, $250,00 is a starting price. A firm price will be provided and approved by the author after the manuscript evaluation and before developmental editing begins.

Complete Publishing Package - $549.00

For authors who want help from manuscript review through publication, Uncommon Door Publishing offers a complete publishing package designed to keep the process affordable, personal, and easy to follow. This package includes manuscript evaluation, copy editing and proof reading, interior formatting, cover design and preparation, publishing setup, distribution assistance, and review of final digital proof before publication. Authors will never be required to approve a finished work they are not comfortable with.

Standard package pricing applies to books up to 75,000 words or 300 finished pages. Projects requiring extensive developmental editing, specialty illustration, complex layouts, or unusual production requirements may require an individual quote. Any additional costs will always be discussed and approved before work begins. CHILDRENS BOOKS, COMICS, AND OTHER WORKS REQUIRING FULL ILLUSTRATION ARE NOT INCLUDED IN THIS CATEGORY.
