After your book is Accepted

Publishing a book is a partnership. Acceptance by Uncommon Door Publishing is the beginning of the process, not the end of it.

Once a manuscript is accepted, we will work with the author to establish a publishing plan. Depending on the needs of the book, this may include revisions, editing, cover development, interior formatting, proof review, publication, distribution, and a practical marketing plan. Nothing goes to print without the authors approval. Authors will have the opportunity to review their cover, interior files, and final proof before publication. Once the book is ready we believe the author should remain part of its journey. Uncommon Door may help coordinate local signings, community events, library opportunities, book fairs, interviews, social-media promotion, and other appropriate opportunities. We will work around an authors reasonable availability when ever possible. We cannot promise best seller lists, national book store placement, a particular number of sales, or media attention. What we can promise is that we will treat your book like a book- not simply a file to be uploaded.

Author Copies & Royalties

Uncoomon Door Publishing believes authors should understand how their book earns money before publication begins. Royalty rates, author-copy costs, retail pricing, and distribution terms will be clearly stated in the authors publishing agreement before the book is released. Authors may purchase copies of their own books for personal sales, signings, events, gifts, or other promotional purposes. The cost of the author copies will depend on the book's format, page count, and printing specifications, and current printing costs.

Books sold through retailers, online bookstores, distributors, or other sales channels may be subject to printing costs, retailer discounts, distribution fees, and other expenses outside of the control of Uncommon Door. These costs will be explained as clearly as possible before publication. There should be no mystery about where the money goes. Authors will know the financial terms of their publishing agreement before they approve publication.

Our Commitment to Authors

Uncommon Door Publishing was created around a simple belief: a writer should not have to be famous, wealthy, or already have a large following for their story to deserve consideration.

We cannot promise that every manuscript will be published, and we cannot promise that every published book will become bestsellers. What we can promise is that we will treat writers with honesty and respect. If we believe a manuscript needs more work , we will say so. If we believe a story deserves an opportunity, we will do our best to help that author find a practical path forward. We believe that publishing should be more than selling a service. There are stories worth telling, writers worth encouraging, and doors worth opening.

About Uncommon Door Publishing

Uncommon Door Publishing began with a simple idea: Good Stories deserve an opportunity. Founded by writer Brandon Bowman, Uncommon Door grew from his own experience navigating the publishing world. He learned firsthand that having a story to tell and finding an open door are two very different things. Uncommon Door was created to make that door a little wider for writers.

Brandon and his wife, Jessica, believe books can entertain while respecting the reader. Their Christian faith shapes the values behind Uncommon Door Publishing, but we are not limited to Christian fiction. We welcome writers from many backgrounds and genres who share our commitment to clean , meaningful storytelling.

Our approach

We are not interested in replacing the individual voice of an author with our own. Whether we are reading a manuscript, helping prepare a book for publication, or standing beside an author at an event, The story still belongs to the storyteller. Uncommon Door Publishing is small by design. We want authors to know who is reading their manuscript, who is working on their book, and who they can talk to when they have a question.

Opening doors to possibilities isnt just our slogan. Its why we're here.
